# Past Gemini / DeepMind Hackathon Winners

The latest SF edition (Gemini 4 Argon, Oct 2026) has its own file: [03-gemini-4-argon-sf-winners.md](03-gemini-4-argon-sf-winners.md).

## Gemini 3 NYC Hackathon: Feb 28, 2026, The Malin Flatiron
The direct predecessor of the event we're entering. Hosted by Google DeepMind and Cerebral Valley. Prize: $100K+ in Gemini API credits plus a call with the founders of Google's AI Futures Fund. 58 projects.

| Place | Project | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **WALL STREET BANANAS** (Hayden Harrow, solo) | A 1980s trading-floor game where you negotiate trades with 22 AI characters, using real 1987–89 market data. Used long context for each character's memory, Nano Banana 2 for sprites, Veo 3.1 for video backgrounds and Lyria 3 for music. |
| 2nd | **What IF?** (Kevin Loo, solo) | A local studio for independent filmmakers: a swarm of AI agents helps with worldbuilding, storyboards and characters, with git version control and a pipeline to video models. |
| 3rd | **ArtLens AI** (Daniel Varoli, Mike Koch) | Makes museum art interactive instead of relying on text placards and audio guides. |
| Finalists | **Beat Drop**, **Coro**, **Syncro** | A rhythm game played on phones with live Lyria music; a collaborative music engine the audience joins by QR code; an AI editor that keeps narration and footage in sync. |

## AIE Code Agents Hackathon, NYC: Nov 2025
Billed as the first official Gemini 3 hackathon. Cerebral Valley's X post names **CodeRalph** as 1st place (not verified on the event page). Judges included swyx and Paige Bailey (DeepMind). Prizes: $6K+ cash and $150K in Gemini credits.

## Gemini 3 Seoul: Feb 28, 2026
1,515 applicants, 219 accepted, 111 projects.
- **1st: GeminiSpace** (Jang Min-su, solo). Turns about eight 360° phone photos into a 2D map, then a 3D voxel map, and answers questions like "what's the route from the fridge to the door?" Used Gemini 3 Flash, Nano Banana and Antigravity.
- **2nd:** Kim Yong-gyu built a self-defending security agent system.

## Sketch & Search, SF then virtual: Nov 2025 (DeepMind × Freepik × Qdrant)
Over $25K in prizes.
- **1st: Prometheus.** Turns a molecule file (PDB) into a cinematic "mechanism trailer" for scientists. Also won Best Use of Gemini.
- **2nd: Roast My Snack.** Turns snack photos into comics with a dental-education angle.
- **3rd: AutoScape.** Turns a photo of a yard into a landscape design with materials and costs. Also won Best Use of Nano Banana.

## Gemini 3 SuperHack, SF: Jan 31, 2026 (sports theme)
Overall winners not found. **Super Analytics**, a live positional analytics tool for coaches, was a finalist and won Stream's sponsor prize.

## Kaggle "Vibe Code with Gemini 3 Pro": Dec 2025, $500K
50 winners across 9 categories, unranked. Examples: NeuroState (firmware design by voice), The Lung Listener, SIMUL-AI-TOR (turns video into a 3D physics lab).

## Patterns among winners
- **Using many Gemini features beats using one.** The NYC winner combined long context, Nano Banana, Veo and Lyria in one product.
- **Teams were small or solo.** Both of the top two in NYC and the Seoul winner worked alone.
- **Spatial and visual work wins.** GeminiSpace, ArtLens and AutoScape all lean on vision.
- **A live, playful demo stands out.** Games, music and interactive experiences took most NYC places.
